About

Welcome to my homepage! My full name is Thanh Duc Pham 👋. I am currently an AI Research Resident at FPT Software AI Center, where I have the privilege of being advised by Prof. Minh-Tan Pham (IRISA, Université Bretagne Sud). Previously, I was a Research Assistant at the Data Science Laboratory (BKAI), advised by Prof. Linh Ngo Van. Email: thanhph29.re@gmail.com

Research Interests

My research focuses on developing principled approaches that enable AI systems to learn and adapt continuously and efficiently. I am particularly interested in DETR-based detection and segmentation models, Knowledge Distillation, Neural Collapse, and Continual Learning, and I am excited to explore related directions that enhance the adaptability and practical impact of modern vision models.

I'm actively seeking graduate (MSc/PhD) positions for the upcoming academic year and excited to collaborate on impactful research! 🚀
